Samsung Galaxy A07 Review: Massive Battery and Reliable Daily Performance Under $150

The Galaxy A07 delivers a big 6.7-inch screen, dependable all-day battery, and the familiar Samsung software experience at a price that typically sits well under $150.

Day 14 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

In the crowded under-$150 smartphone space, the Samsung Galaxy A07 stands out by sticking to practical fundamentals. Frequently available around $130 on Amazon in unlocked configurations, it pairs a large display with a battery built for long days and the polished One UI interface that Samsung fans already know.

The 6.7-inch HD+ screen with a 90Hz refresh rate handles everyday scrolling, video, and messaging smoothly. It is not the sharpest panel in its class, yet it stays bright enough for indoor use and feels responsive during normal tasks. The slim plastic body keeps the phone light and easy to handle one-handed for most people.

Battery life is the clearest strength. With a 5000mAh cell and efficient 4G hardware, most users report easily clearing a full day of mixed use, and light users often stretch into a second day. Charging is straightforward via USB-C, and the phone includes a headphone jack for those who still prefer wired audio.

Samsung’s software approach adds real value here. One UI remains relatively clean and well-optimized on this hardware, with useful features like Circle to Search on supported builds, a reliable fingerprint reader, and a multi-year update commitment that is uncommon at this price. Storage starts at 64GB or 128GB depending on the variant and expands via microSD.

The dual-camera setup (50MP main) is competent in good light for social photos and documents, though low-light results remain average. If you want a no-nonsense 4G phone that prioritizes battery, familiarity, and everyday reliability without spending more than $150, the Galaxy A07 is a straightforward recommendation.

Key Takeaways

  • Price Range: ~$120 – $140 (Amazon)
  • Display: 6.7-inch HD+ 90Hz
  • Battery: 5,000mAh for all-day use
  • Features: One UI, expandable storage, dual SIM, IP54 splash resistance, headphone jack
