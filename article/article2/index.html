Samsung Galaxy A16 5G Review: AMOLED and Long Support Under $150

The unlocked 128GB Galaxy A16 5G brings a bright Super AMOLED screen, real 5G, and years of updates to a price most budget phones still miss.

Day 8 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

Most phones under $150 still ask you to pick between a decent screen and a phone you can keep. The Samsung Galaxy A16 5G, in the common 4GB/128GB unlocked configuration, often lands around $135 to $148 and refuses that trade. It is a current Samsung, not a leftover, and that matters when the alternative is a no-name handset with a mystery update plan.

The 6.7-inch Super AMOLED panel is the headline. Full HD+ resolution at 90Hz, with about 800 nits in high-brightness mode, makes scrolling, maps, and video look richer than the LCD panels that still dominate this price. Colors stay punchy indoors, and outdoor readability is better than older Galaxy A-series screens. A side fingerprint reader and IP54 splash resistance keep the daily basics covered without pretending this is a rugged flagship.

Battery life is the other practical win. The 5,000mAh cell pairs with 25W wired charging, so a typical day of messages, social apps, and streaming is comfortable, and many owners stretch into a second morning. Charging is not class-leading, but 25W is fast enough that a short top-up before leaving the house is actually useful. 5G on the Dimensity 6300 or Exynos 1330, depending on region, is steady for everyday apps rather than heavy gaming.

Cameras stay in budget territory and that is fine if you know the brief. The 50MP main sensor handles daylight and social shots well, with a 5MP ultrawide and 2MP macro that are more extra than essential. The 13MP front camera is competent for calls and quick selfies. Storage starts at 128GB and expands with microSD, which is still a real advantage when cloud plans are not part of the budget.

Software is where Samsung pulls ahead of similar Motorola and store-brand phones. The A16 5G is promised up to six major Android updates from its Android 14 base, plus a long security window. Performance with 4GB of RAM is fine for one or two apps at a time and can feel tight if you keep many tabs open, so this is a daily driver, not a power user phone. For buyers who want a bright screen, 5G, and a phone that will still get patches in a few years, it is one of the clearest picks under $150.

Key Takeaways

  • Price Range: ~$135 – $148 for the unlocked 128GB model (Amazon)
  • Display: 6.7-inch Super AMOLED, 90Hz, Full HD+
  • Battery: 5,000mAh with 25W wired charging
  • Support: 5G, 50MP main camera, microSD, up to six OS updates
