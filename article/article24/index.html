Amazfit Active 2 Review: GPS Maps and Long Battery Under $150

At around $100, the Active 2 delivers a bright round AMOLED, accurate GPS with free offline maps, and a battery that easily lasts a week for most users.

Day 24 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The Amazfit Active 2 stands out in the crowded under-$150 smartwatch space by packing features that usually sit higher up the price ladder. The round stainless-steel case with a 1.32-inch AMOLED display looks clean on the wrist, and the screen hits up to 2,000 nits so outdoor visibility stays solid even in direct sun.

Battery life is the everyday win. Amazfit rates it for up to 10 days of typical use with the always-on display off, and real-world mixed use (notifications, heart-rate checks, a few workouts) routinely lands in the 5–7 day range. Continuous GPS use drops that further, but for most people this is still a charge-once-a-week watch rather than a nightly habit.

Tracking is more complete than many rivals at this price. Built-in multi-GNSS GPS locks quickly, and free offline maps with turn-by-turn directions via the Zepp app mean you can leave the phone behind on runs or hikes. Heart-rate accuracy is solid for steady efforts, sleep tracking is detailed, and there are 160+ sport modes including strength and HYROX. 5 ATM water resistance covers swimming and daily wear.

Software runs on Zepp OS, which keeps things light and focused on health and fitness rather than a full app store. Notifications and quick replies work well on both Android and iPhone. The main limits are the lack of NFC payments on the standard model and the fact that it is not Wear OS or watchOS. For pure fitness tracking and a long-lasting screen, those trade-offs are easy to accept.

If you want a stylish, capable fitness watch that does not demand daily charging or a premium price, the Amazfit Active 2 is one of the strongest current options under $150. It will not replace a full smartwatch for apps or tap-to-pay, but for steps, routes, sleep, and a wrist you mostly forget about, it delivers real value.
