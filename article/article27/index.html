Xiaomi Redmi Watch 6 Review: 24-Day Battery and Bright AMOLED Under $150

Often available around $90–$95, the Redmi Watch 6 delivers a large, bright 2.07-inch AMOLED screen, solid GPS tracking, and multi-week battery life that makes it one of the strongest values in the budget smartwatch category.

Day 27 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The Xiaomi Redmi Watch 6 stands out among sub-$150 smartwatches with its unusually large and bright display. The 2.07-inch AMOLED panel reaches high brightness levels for outdoor readability and pairs with a slim aluminum frame that keeps the watch light on the wrist. Customizable faces and a clean interface make checking stats or notifications quick and simple.

Battery life is exceptional. Xiaomi rates it for up to 24 days of typical use, and even with regular GPS workouts most owners report easily clearing two weeks between charges. That endurance is a major practical advantage over watches that need nightly charging. The magnetic charger is straightforward and reliable.

Built-in dual-band GNSS provides accurate outdoor tracking for runs, walks, and rides without needing a phone. Bluetooth calling and message replies further reduce the need to pull out a phone mid-activity. The watch supports more than 150 workout modes along with continuous heart rate, blood oxygen, sleep stages, and stress monitoring. Data syncs cleanly to the Mi Fitness app on both Android and iOS.

Build quality is solid for the price: a lightweight aluminum case, 5 ATM water resistance suitable for swimming and showering, and a comfortable silicone strap. The software is responsive for everyday use, though it lacks the deep third-party app ecosystem of Wear OS or watchOS. For pure fitness tracking, notifications, and long wear time, it covers the essentials well.

At roughly $90–$95 (frequently discounted), the Redmi Watch 6 offers one of the best combinations of screen size, battery endurance, GPS accuracy, and features available under $150. It is an excellent choice for anyone who wants capable tracking without daily charging or a premium price tag.
