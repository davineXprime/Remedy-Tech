Amazfit Bip 6 Review: Bright AMOLED and Two-Week Battery Under $150

Often available around $80, the Amazfit Bip 6 delivers a large vibrant AMOLED screen, solid fitness tracking, and exceptional battery life that outlasts most rivals in the budget range.

Day 29 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The Amazfit Bip 6 stands out under $150 thanks to its 1.97-inch AMOLED display. Colors are rich, blacks are deep, and the 2000-nit peak brightness keeps the screen readable outdoors. The squared-off design feels modern without being overly bulky for most wrists, and the aluminum frame adds a touch of durability.

Battery life is the headline feature. Amazfit rates it for up to 14 days of typical use, and real-world testing often lands in the 6–10 day range even with GPS workouts and always-on display enabled. That is a major upgrade over daily-charge smartwatches and means fewer interruptions for charging.

Fitness tracking covers the basics well. Built-in GPS supports outdoor runs and walks with offline map support, while more than 140 sport modes cover everything from swimming to strength training. Heart-rate monitoring, sleep tracking, and SpO2 readings are reliable enough for daily use, though advanced metrics are more limited than premium devices.

Software is straightforward via the Zepp app. Notifications, Bluetooth calling, and basic music controls work across Android and iOS. The interface is clean and responsive, with regular updates improving features over time. It is not a full Wear OS experience, but for most people the trade-off is worth the battery and price savings.

At roughly $80 (frequently lower on sale), the Bip 6 offers excellent value for anyone who wants a capable daily tracker without spending flagship money. It is a practical pick for fitness beginners, budget shoppers, or anyone tired of charging a watch every night.
