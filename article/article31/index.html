Amazfit Bip 6 Review: Long Battery and Bright AMOLED Under $150

Often found around $80, the Amazfit Bip 6 delivers a vivid 1.97-inch AMOLED screen, multi-day battery life, and solid fitness tracking that punches above its budget price.

Day 31 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The Amazfit Bip 6 stands out in the sub-$100 smartwatch category with its 1.97-inch AMOLED display. Colors pop and outdoor readability is strong thanks to peak brightness around 2,000 nits, making metrics easy to glance at during runs or gym sessions without squinting.

Battery life is the headline feature. Amazfit claims up to 14 days of typical use, and real-world testing often yields a solid week even with daily GPS workouts and always-on display enabled. That longevity means less frequent charging compared to many rivals that need a top-up every few days.

Fitness tracking covers the essentials well: heart rate, sleep stages, SpO2, and over 140 sport modes. Built-in GPS with offline map support is a practical bonus for outdoor activities, letting you leave the phone behind on runs or hikes. The Zepp app handles data syncing cleanly for both Android and iOS.

Design is lightweight and sport-focused with a square aluminum-alloy case and comfortable silicone band. It includes Bluetooth calling and text notifications, plus 5 ATM water resistance for swimming. Software is straightforward via Zepp OS, though the app ecosystem is more limited than premium watches.

At roughly $80 on Amazon, the Bip 6 offers excellent value for anyone wanting reliable daily tracking and long battery without flagship pricing. It is especially appealing if you prioritize display quality and endurance over advanced smart features like full app stores or contactless payments.
