Amazfit Bip 6 Review: Vibrant AMOLED and Two-Week Battery Under $150

Frequently listed around $79 on Amazon, the Amazfit Bip 6 delivers a large bright AMOLED screen, exceptional battery endurance, onboard GPS with offline maps, and practical calling features at a fraction of flagship prices.

Day 35 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The 1.97-inch AMOLED display is the clear highlight. At 390 × 450 resolution and peak brightness up to 2,000 nits, colors pop and text stays readable outdoors. The always-on option and ambient light sensor keep the face usable without constant tapping, while the square form with rounded corners and aluminum frame feels modern without looking cheap.

Battery life is where the Bip 6 consistently over-delivers. Amazfit rates it for up to 14 days of typical use and around 6 days under heavier loads that include GPS workouts. Real-world tests often see a full week or more even with notifications, heart-rate monitoring, and occasional GPS sessions, far outlasting most Wear OS or Apple Watch alternatives in this price range. Charging is straightforward and the 340 mAh cell is efficient thanks to the lightweight Zepp OS.

Fitness tracking covers more than 140 sport modes, built-in dual-band GNSS for independent GPS, and offline maps so you can navigate routes without your phone. Heart-rate, SpO2, sleep stages, and stress monitoring are solid for daily insights. Bluetooth calling and text replies (via the Zepp app) work well for quick check-ins, and the 5 ATM water resistance handles swimming and showers without worry.

Software is clean and lightweight. The companion app on Android and iOS surfaces clear data, customizable watch faces, and notifications. There is no full app store or NFC payments, but for most users the built-in features and long battery more than compensate. The replaceable silicone strap is comfortable, and the overall weight stays low enough for all-day wear.

At prices that frequently sit well under $150—often around $79—the Amazfit Bip 6 is one of the strongest pure-value smartwatches available. It prioritizes the things that matter most for budget buyers: a vibrant screen, multi-day (or multi-week) battery, reliable GPS, and practical health tracking without forcing daily charging.
