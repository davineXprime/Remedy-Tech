Samsung Galaxy A16 5G Review: Bright AMOLED and Solid 5G Under $150

Frequently listed around $130 on Amazon, the Galaxy A16 5G brings a large 90Hz AMOLED screen, capable 50MP camera, long battery life, and six years of updates to the sub-$150 range.

Day 36 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The 6.7-inch Super AMOLED display is the standout feature. With a 90Hz refresh rate and solid brightness, scrolling feels smooth and colors stay vivid even outdoors. Text is sharp, and the punch-hole camera cutout is minimal. For the price, this is one of the better panels available, making media and everyday use more enjoyable than typical LCD budget phones.

Battery life holds up well thanks to the 5000mAh cell. Most users can expect a full day of moderate use with room to spare, and lighter users often stretch into a second day. Fast charging gets it back to usable levels quickly, and the efficiency of the mid-range chipset helps keep drain in check during normal tasks like messaging, browsing, and streaming.

The 50MP main camera performs respectably in daylight, capturing decent detail and natural colors for social sharing and casual photos. Low-light results are softer, as expected at this price, but the system is reliable for everyday snapshots. Storage is expandable via microSD, and the base 128GB configuration gives plenty of room for apps and media without immediately feeling cramped.

Software is clean One UI on Android, with Samsung’s promise of six years of OS and security updates—an unusually long commitment in the budget segment. 5G connectivity is solid across major bands, and the phone feels snappy for web browsing, social apps, and light multitasking. Build quality is plastic but sturdy, with IP54-level resistance on many variants for basic dust and splash protection.

At prices that frequently sit well under $150—often around $120–$140—the Galaxy A16 5G delivers a balanced, no-nonsense experience. It prioritizes the essentials budget buyers care about most: a bright high-refresh screen, dependable battery, usable camera, modern connectivity, and long software support without requiring a flagship budget.
