Amazfit Bip 6 Review: Long Battery and Bright AMOLED Under $150

Frequently listed around $79 on Amazon, the Amazfit Bip 6 delivers a large vibrant AMOLED screen, multi-week battery life, built-in GPS, and comprehensive fitness tracking without the premium price tag.

Day 37 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The 1.97-inch AMOLED display is one of the best features at this price. It stays bright and colorful indoors and remains readable outdoors in most conditions, making glanceable stats and workout data easy to check. The square design with rounded corners feels modern, and the lightweight aluminum-and-polymer build keeps it comfortable for all-day wear without looking overly bulky.

Battery life is the standout strength. Amazfit claims up to 14 days with typical use, and real-world testing often delivers a full week even with GPS workouts and notifications enabled. Heavier users with always-on display still clear several days easily. This is a major advantage over premium smartwatches that demand nightly charging, especially for people who want reliable tracking without constant power management.

Built-in GPS with free offline maps supports outdoor activities cleanly, while the 140+ sports modes cover everything from running and cycling to strength training and more niche workouts. Heart rate, sleep stages, stress, and SpO2 tracking work consistently for everyday health monitoring. Bluetooth calling and text replies add practical smart features without complicating the experience.

Software via the Zepp app is straightforward and receives regular updates. The watch pairs cleanly with both Android and iPhone, and the interface stays responsive for basic navigation and notifications. Water resistance (typically 5 ATM) handles rain and swimming without issue. Build quality feels solid for the price, with a comfortable silicone strap that holds up to daily activity.

At prices that frequently sit well under $150—often around $70–$80—the Amazfit Bip 6 prioritizes the features budget buyers value most: a bright high-resolution screen, exceptional battery endurance, accurate GPS tracking, and broad fitness tools without requiring a flagship budget or ecosystem lock-in.
