Amazfit Active 2 Review: Stylish AMOLED GPS Watch Under $150

Frequently listed around $85–$100 on Amazon, the Amazfit Active 2 delivers a premium-looking stainless steel case, bright AMOLED screen, independent GPS with offline maps, and solid fitness tracking that outperforms its price.

Day 40 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The 1.32-inch AMOLED display stands out for its brightness and clarity. Peak brightness reaches up to 2,000 nits, making it easy to read outdoors, while the circular design and stainless steel case give the Active 2 a more refined appearance than many rectangular budget watches. It pairs well with both silicone and leather-style straps for everyday or casual wear.

Battery life is practical for real use. Amazfit rates the Active 2 for up to 10 days of typical wear, and most users report 7–10 days even with notifications and occasional GPS sessions. That reduces the daily charging habit common with more feature-heavy smartwatches. 5 ATM water resistance handles swimming and showers without issue.

Built-in GPS with support for offline maps lets you track outdoor activities independently. The watch includes over 160 workout modes, covering running, cycling, strength training, and even specialized options like HYROX. Heart rate, SpO2, sleep, and stress monitoring provide useful daily trends, with BioTracker sensors that perform reliably for casual fitness rather than elite training.

Bluetooth calling works through the speaker and mic, and notifications arrive promptly once paired. The Zepp app handles data review, watch face customization, and basic coaching without heavy bloat. Compatibility covers both Android and iOS, and the software stays responsive for the essentials even if it lacks the full app ecosystem of Wear OS.

At prices that frequently sit well under $150—often around $85–$100—the Amazfit Active 2 prioritizes what most budget buyers want: a stylish design, bright always-readable screen, independent GPS, and multi-day battery life that supports consistent wear without constant attention.
