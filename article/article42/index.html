Amazfit Active 2 Review: Compact AMOLED GPS Watch Under $150

Frequently priced around $99–$130 on Amazon, the Amazfit Active 2 delivers a bright round AMOLED screen, onboard GPS, multi-day battery, and comprehensive health metrics in a lightweight package that punches above its budget.

Day 42 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The 1.32-inch round AMOLED display is one of the Active 2’s biggest wins. It is sharp, colorful, and bright enough for outdoor use, making maps, notifications, and workout stats easy to read at a glance. The circular form factor feels more traditional and premium than many square budget watches, while remaining slim and comfortable for all-day wear.

Battery life is excellent for the category. Amazfit rates it for up to 10 days of typical use, and real-world mixed tracking with occasional GPS workouts still clears several days easily. This reduces charging anxiety compared with many rivals that demand nightly top-ups, especially when you are traveling or training consistently.

Fitness and health tracking are thorough. Built-in GPS supports accurate outdoor runs and rides without needing a phone, while heart-rate, SpO2, sleep, stress, and dozens of sport modes cover everyday activity. Accuracy is solid for a sub-$150 device, and the Zepp app provides clear summaries and trends that help you stay consistent without overwhelming you with data.

Smart features keep it practical. You get call and message notifications, basic app interactions, and Bluetooth calling on supported models, plus a clean interface that stays responsive. The aluminum case and silicone strap are durable enough for workouts and daily life, and 5ATM water resistance handles swimming and showers without worry.

Value is where the Active 2 shines brightest. At typical street prices well under $150, it offers a combination of display quality, GPS, battery endurance, and tracking depth that used to require spending significantly more. It is not a full Wear OS replacement, but for most people seeking a reliable fitness companion it delivers more than enough without the premium price tag.
