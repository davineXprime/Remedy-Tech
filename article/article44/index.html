CMF Watch 3 Pro Review: Stylish AMOLED GPS Watch Under $150

Frequently available around $79–$99 on Amazon, the CMF Watch 3 Pro delivers a bright 1.43-inch AMOLED screen, dual-band GPS, multi-day battery, and a clean interface in a distinctive metal-framed design that stands out in the budget segment.

Day 44 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The 1.43-inch round AMOLED display is sharp and colorful at 466 × 466 resolution, with peak brightness around 670 nits that holds up reasonably well outdoors. Always-on display support and over 120 watch faces let you match the look to your style, while the rotating crown makes navigation feel precise and premium for the price. It is larger and more refined than many square budget watches.

Battery life is a clear strength. Rated for up to 13 days of typical use (or roughly 4–5 days with AOD and heavier activity), it easily outlasts most smartwatches in this range and reduces the need for nightly charging. Full charges take about 90–100 minutes, and real-world mixed use with occasional GPS still clears a full week for many people.

Tracking and connectivity cover the essentials well. Dual-band L1+L5 GPS improves outdoor accuracy for runs and rides without relying on your phone, while heart-rate, SpO2, sleep stages, stress, and 130+ sport modes handle daily fitness needs. Bluetooth calling, notifications, and basic smart features work reliably via the Nothing X app on both Android and iOS, with a clean, responsive interface that avoids clutter.

Build quality matches the distinctive look. The metal frame and IP68 rating handle daily wear and light water exposure, while swappable silicone straps add flexibility. It is not a full Wear OS device with third-party apps or NFC payments, and swimming is not recommended, but for notifications, workouts, and health metrics it delivers a polished experience without the usual budget compromises in design.

Value is exceptional for anyone who wants style and practical features without overspending. At typical prices well under $150 (often near $80–$100), the CMF Watch 3 Pro combines a standout AMOLED screen, solid GPS, long battery, and recognizable brand polish that many generic alternatives lack. It is an easy recommendation for budget-conscious users who prioritize looks and everyday reliability.
