Amazfit GTS 4 Mini Review: Compact AMOLED and Long Battery Under $150

Frequently listed around $90–$120 on Amazon, the GTS 4 Mini delivers a bright square AMOLED screen, multi-day battery life, built-in GPS, and comprehensive health tracking in a lightweight package that stays well under the budget ceiling.

Day 45 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The 1.65-inch AMOLED display is one of the strongest points at this price. It offers sharp resolution, vibrant colors, and strong brightness for outdoor visibility, making glanceable fitness data and notifications easy to read without squinting. The square form factor feels modern and compact on smaller wrists, while the always-on option is available if you prefer constant visibility (at a modest battery cost).

Battery life is excellent for the category. Amazfit rates the GTS 4 Mini for up to 15 days of typical use, and real-world mixed wear with notifications, heart-rate monitoring, and occasional GPS workouts often lands in the 8–12 day range. That means far fewer charges than most smartwatches, which is a practical win for anyone who wants consistent tracking without daily plug-ins.

Fitness and health features cover the essentials well. Built-in GPS tracks outdoor runs and rides accurately enough for most users, with support for multiple satellite systems. Heart rate, SpO2, sleep stages, stress, and more than 120 sport modes provide useful daily insights. The lightweight build and 5 ATM water resistance make it comfortable for all-day wear and swimming.

Software runs on Zepp OS, which keeps the interface clean and focused on health data rather than heavy apps. Notifications and basic interactions work smoothly with both Android and iPhone. The companion Zepp app offers coaching insights and watch-face options without forcing subscriptions. Limitations include no full third-party app store and limited on-watch replies in some setups, but for fitness-first users these are easy trade-offs.

At prices that frequently sit well under $150—often around $100—the Amazfit GTS 4 Mini prioritizes what budget buyers actually need: a high-quality AMOLED screen in a compact form, strong multi-day battery endurance, independent GPS tracking, and reliable health sensors that support consistent use without premium pricing or daily charging.
