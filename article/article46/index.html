Xiaomi Redmi 15 Review: Massive Battery and Smooth 144Hz Display Under $150

Frequently listed around $120–$140 on Amazon, the Redmi 15 pairs a large high-refresh screen with an unusually large 7000mAh battery, delivering multi-day endurance and fluid everyday use without breaking the budget.

Day 46 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The 6.9-inch FHD+ IPS LCD is the standout feature for the price. It supports up to 144Hz adaptive refresh, which makes scrolling, browsing, and light gaming feel noticeably smoother than the 90Hz panels common in this range. Brightness reaches levels suitable for outdoor viewing, and the punch-hole design keeps the large screen immersive. While it is an LCD rather than AMOLED, the combination of size and high refresh rate delivers excellent value for media consumption.

Battery life is exceptional. The 7000mAh silicon-carbon cell is among the largest available under $150 and routinely delivers two full days of moderate use, or a full day-plus with heavier streaming and navigation. 33W charging is faster than many budget rivals, and reverse charging is a welcome bonus for topping up earbuds or another device in a pinch. Real-world endurance is the clearest practical advantage here.

Performance is handled by the Snapdragon 685, which is well-suited to everyday tasks, social apps, video streaming, and casual games. Paired with 6GB or 8GB of RAM depending on the variant and 128GB of expandable storage, the phone stays responsive for typical use. HyperOS on Android 15 keeps the interface clean and includes useful optimizations without excessive bloat for the segment.

The dual-camera setup is led by a 50MP main sensor that produces solid daylight photos with good detail and natural colors. An 8MP front camera handles selfies and video calls adequately. Video tops out at 1080p, which is expected at this price, but the results are usable for social sharing. Build quality includes IP64 splash resistance, making it more durable than basic budget phones.

At prices that frequently sit well under $150, the Xiaomi Redmi 15 prioritizes what budget buyers actually need: a large smooth high-refresh display, class-leading battery endurance, reliable everyday performance, and enough camera capability for daily photography—all without the premium markup of higher-end models.
