Amazfit Bip 6 Review: Long Battery and GPS Maps Under $150

Frequently listed around $65–$80 on Amazon, the Amazfit Bip 6 delivers a bright 1.97-inch AMOLED screen, multi-week battery life, accurate GPS with free offline maps, and Bluetooth calling in a lightweight package well under $150.

Day 48 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The 1.97-inch AMOLED display is one of the strongest points. It offers sharp, vibrant colors and solid outdoor visibility, making fitness metrics and notifications easy to read at a glance. The always-on option is available if you prefer it, and the square design with rounded corners keeps the watch looking modern without feeling bulky on the wrist.

Battery life is excellent for the price. Amazfit rates it for up to 14 days of typical use, and real-world mixed tracking usually lands in the 10–12 day range even with GPS workouts and notifications enabled. That kind of endurance removes the daily charging ritual common to more expensive smartwatches and is a major practical advantage under $150.

Built-in dual-band GPS with free offline maps stands out in this price range. Route tracking is reliable for runs, rides, and hikes, and the offline maps help with navigation without draining your phone. Bluetooth calling and text replies (with quick responses) add convenience when your phone is nearby, while the 140+ workout modes cover everything from strength training to outdoor sports with automatic recognition for common activities.

Health tracking includes continuous heart rate, SpO2, sleep stages, stress, and a readiness score. Accuracy is solid for everyday insights rather than clinical precision, which is expected at this price. The companion app is straightforward, and 5 ATM water resistance covers swimming and daily wear without worry. Build quality feels durable for a lightweight aluminum-frame design.

At prices that frequently sit well under $150 (often closer to $70), the Amazfit Bip 6 prioritizes long battery life, useful GPS with maps, a bright AMOLED screen, and broad fitness features—making it one of the strongest budget smartwatch values currently available.
