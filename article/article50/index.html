Motorola Moto G 2025 Review: Smooth 120Hz and Solid Battery Under $150

Frequently listed around $140–$150 on Amazon (unlocked configurations), the Moto G 2025 brings a large 6.7-inch 120Hz display, reliable 50MP camera, 5000mAh battery, and Motorola’s near-stock Android into easy under-$150 territory.

Day 50 Spotlight • Amazon 4 min read

The 6.7-inch LCD display with 120Hz refresh rate is the everyday highlight. Scrolling and animations feel fluid, which is uncommon at this price, and the panel is bright enough for indoor use with decent outdoor readability. It is not an AMOLED, so contrast is more modest, but the high refresh rate and large size make media and daily tasks comfortable without feeling oversized.

Battery life centers on a 5000mAh cell that typically delivers a full day of mixed use and often stretches further with lighter activity. TurboPower 30W charging provides quick top-ups when needed. Combined with the efficient Dimensity 6300 chipset, the phone avoids the mid-afternoon anxiety common in many budget devices, making it a practical daily driver.

The 50MP main camera handles everyday shots with solid detail and natural color in good light. Low-light results are usable rather than class-leading, which is expected here, while the additional sensors cover basic needs. The front camera is fine for video calls and social posts. Motorola’s software processing stays straightforward without aggressive oversharpening.

Software is one of the clearer strengths. Near-stock Android with Motorola’s light customizations keeps the experience clean and responsive, free of heavy bloat. Expandable storage via microSD, 5G connectivity, dual SIM support, and a headphone jack add practical utility. The vegan-leather back gives a more premium tactile feel than typical plastic, and basic splash resistance covers everyday accidents. Build quality is solid for the money.

At prices that frequently sit under $150 on Amazon, the Moto G 2025 prioritizes a smooth high-refresh display, respectable battery endurance, clean software, and everyday camera performance—making it a dependable budget smartphone for anyone who wants reliable Motorola simplicity without flagship cost.
