CMF Watch Pro: Minimalist Design, Maximum Value

A bold, geometric smartwatch offering a massive 1.96-inch AMOLED display and built-in multi-system GPS for just $69.

Archive • Amazon 3 min read

CMF by Nothing brings extreme minimalism to the affordable smartwatch space. The Watch Pro avoids decorative clutter and instead presents a clean industrial aesthetic that feels premium without trying too hard to look expensive.

The standout feature is the huge 1.96-inch AMOLED display. It dominates the face of the watch and looks bright enough for everyday visibility, even outdoors. That alone gives the device a more premium impression than its modest price would suggest.

Functionally, the Watch Pro covers the essentials well. It tracks heart rate, sleep, stress, and general activity, while also including built-in GPS for outdoor workouts. For a watch in this range, the presence of accurate route tracking and Bluetooth calling makes it especially compelling.

The interface is straightforward, and the overall design language suits buyers who want a smartwatch that feels understated but distinctive. It does not attempt to match the deeper ecosystem features of flagship wearables, but it absolutely delivers on the basics for a very reasonable price.

If your goal is a smartwatch with style, utility, and a bold display that feels more expensive than it is, the CMF Watch Pro is a strong value pick.

Key Takeaways

  • Massive 1.96-inch bright AMOLED screen
  • Striking industrial design and stylized UI
  • Built-in GPS and Bluetooth calling for under $70
  • Great value for buyers who want a practical everyday wearable
