When searching Amazon for a capable smartwatch under $100, buyers often encounter bare-bones fitness trackers disguised as full smartwatches. The Amazfit GTS 4 Mini breaks that trend by offering a crisp HD AMOLED display, standalone GPS tracking, and a comprehensive biometric suite for under $90. Price range is ~$89–$99 on Amazon Prime with a 1.65-inch HD AMOLED screen.

Battery life lasts up to 15 days on typical use and 45 days on battery saver. Sensors include BioTracker 3.0 PPG with continuous SpO2, heart rate, and stress tracking. GPS uses 5 satellite positioning systems for accurate outdoor tracking.

Weighing under 20 grams without the strap, the GTS 4 Mini features over 120 sports modes with automatic workout detection. Its integrated multi-satellite GPS enables runners and cyclists to record precise route maps without needing a tethered smartphone. With 24-hour continuous heart rate monitoring, blood oxygen saturation tracking, and detailed sleep-stage analysis, it delivers vital wellness insights comparable to wristwear costing twice as much.
