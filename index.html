Why Tri-Fuel Technology is Essential for Modern Outage Protection

When major storms, hurricanes, or winter freezes disrupt the power grid, traditional gasoline generators often face gas station closures and long fuel lines.

The Generac 12,500 Starting Watt Tri-Fuel Generator solves this problem by allowing homeowners to run on Natural Gas, Liquid Propane, or Gasoline.

Natural Gas (NG)

Hooks directly to residential gas lines for unlimited continuous fuel during long outages.

Liquid Propane (LP)

Clean fuel that stores indefinitely in tanks without degrading or clogging engine parts.

Standard Gasoline

Provides maximum 12,500 starting watt output whenever high peak power demand is needed.
